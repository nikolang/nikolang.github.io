# se-maps

Map tiles for the VRChat world "Suborbital Express", derived from the open data
sets below.

**Satellite imagery (surface albedo)**
Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2023, 2024.

**Night lights**
Black Marble nighttime lights: Román, M.O. et al. (2018), NASA's Black Marble
nighttime lights product suite, Remote Sensing of Environment 210, 113–143.
Imagery provided by services from NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS),
part of NASA's Earth Observing System Data and Information System (EOSDIS).

**Terrain elevation**
Copernicus DEM: © DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH
2014–2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights
reserved.
World height map (`Earth_bump_map*.png`): NOAA National Centers for
Environmental Information (2022), ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model,
DOI 10.25921/fd45-gt74. Public domain (U.S. Government work).
